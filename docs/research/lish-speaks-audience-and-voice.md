# Lish Speaks audience and editorial voice

Research date: 2026-09-07. Supports [decision issue #8](https://github.com/jimmyblain/podcast-processing/issues/8). The owner approved the audience statement and description voice on 2026-09-07. Other recommendations and profile candidates remain proposals; this is not an implemented publishing contract.

## Finding

The strongest positioning is faith-rooted encouragement for people pursuing purpose in their work, creativity, relationships, and everyday lives. The show's stated mission addresses people taking initiative and finding their own way, spanning career, entrepreneurship, identity, and personal growth. [Official podcast library](https://www.illjustletmyselfin.com/)

Lish directly identifies entrepreneurial, imaginative, independently minded people who want to make a difference as intended listeners. [Communication episode, opening transcript](https://www.illjustletmyselfin.com/2329480/episodes/19492018-5-communication-pillars-that-saved-my-relationships)

**Approved default audience statement (2026-09-07):**

> People ready to take a chance on themselves—in creative work, careers, relationships, and personal growth—who welcome honest conversation, practical encouragement, and a Christian perspective.

**Proposed listener outcome — recommendation:**

> Leave feeling understood, encouraged, and challenged, with a concrete next step they can apply to their own life.

No private audience analytics were available. Age, gender proportions, race, income, geography, and denomination remain unknown. The homepage mentions speaking to women and young people; that does not establish a women-only or youth-only podcast audience. [Host homepage](https://www.lishspeaks.com/)

## Approved description voice

**Warm and familiar; candid and challenging; practical; faith-grounded; playful and human.** Speak directly to the listener, pair encouragement with honest questions, explain what the conversation helps them think through or do, preserve Christian themes when they occur in the episode, and allow humor and vulnerability without forcing slang. Approved by the owner on 2026-09-07. This is an editorial synthesis of the sources above, including the host biography and marriage transcript. It is not a claim that every existing description follows one consistent style.

| Do (recommendation) | Avoid (recommendation) |
|---|---|
| Open with a recognizable question, tension, or specific moment from the episode. | Generic praise that could introduce any guest or episode. |
| Address the reader naturally; use everyday language and contractions. | Corporate biography language or forced slang. |
| Explain what the conversation covers and why it may matter to the listener. | Promising a life change, cure, or result the episode cannot substantiate. |
| Keep faith language where it reflects the conversation; explain unfamiliar references when needed. | Adding scripture, doctrine, or spiritual claims absent from the recording or approved notes. |
| Make room for vulnerability, humor, and honest challenge. | Shame, invented controversy, or exaggerated conflict. |
| Be specific about the guest's story and practical insights. | Treating fame or credentials as the entire reason to watch. |
| Preserve real wording sparingly when useful and verified. | Inventing catchphrases or impersonating Lish's first-person experiences. |

Existing notes use both first-person and third-person framing, paragraphs and topic lists, and varying amounts of promotional emphasis. These are observed practices, not automatically the desired generator template. For example, the Tiara notes use a question-led hook, while the Erica notes begin by identifying the guest and conversation. [Tiara episode](https://www.illjustletmyselfin.com/2329480/episodes/19589071-how-to-be-a-christian-influencer-without-being-preachy-into-influencing-w-tiara-jones), [Erica episode](https://www.illjustletmyselfin.com/2329480/episodes/19633928-why-you-still-feel-like-you-have-something-to-prove-i-ll-just-let-myself-in-w-erica-campbell)

**Illustrative new copy, not a host quote or finalized template:**

> Have you ever said what you meant and still felt misunderstood? Lish explores five communication practices for expressing your needs, listening with care, and building healthier relationships, with personal stories and a faith perspective on finding the words.

This example is grounded in the topics of the communication episode; it does not claim that the practices guarantee a particular outcome. [Episode](https://www.illjustletmyselfin.com/2329480/episodes/19492018-5-communication-pillars-that-saved-my-relationships)

## Method and limits

Read the supplied host site, its directly linked podcast library, five representative official episode pages, and available transcript excerpts. The parent research session inspected the exact supplied YouTube playlist in the browser. Sampling covered solo advice, creative/faith interviews, a music interview, and a personal relationship conversation; it was qualitative, not a statistical content audit. No complete audio listening or private analytics review was performed.

Published transcripts contain visible recognition and speaker-label errors, so they were used to infer broad themes and conversational character, not to establish names or precise quotations. Existing marketing copy was treated as evidence of current presentation, not an approved quality benchmark. This is one-time research requested for issue #8; it does not imply automatic web research for each future episode.
